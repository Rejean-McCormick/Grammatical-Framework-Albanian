# C10-DEC-001 — Materialize G1/G2/G3 without claiming execution

Status: ACCEPTED IMPLEMENTATION DECISION

The certification workstream materializes deterministic campaign manifests and executable Wordbench scenarios for G1=569, G2=5,000 and G3=20,000. Materialization does not change maturity. A CASE counts as executed only after exact normalized GF/Wordbench output exists, and as reviewed only after a hash-bound linguistic verdict exists.

G2 keeps the 500 core CASEs and expands lexical sampling. G3 is a strict superset and adds 15,000 detailed morphology-cell probes drawn preferentially from exact/baseline entries. Compile-safe placeholders are not selected for G3 detailed protected-depth probes.
